About

I've been building for the web for over twelve years — long enough to have been the junior developer, the team leader, the project manager, and now the full stack engineer. Each of those jobs is harder than it looks from the outside, and having done all of them is the thing I value most about my career.

I started at PT Walden Global Services in Bandung writing Drupal and Laravel, moved to PT Smoets Teknologi Outsourcing where I grew from lead developer to team leader to IT project manager, and in 2021 I made a deliberate choice: back to full-time engineering. Since then I've been at 99 Group, working on the Rumah123 web platform — lead generation flows, user tracking, ads monetization, and the unglamorous refactoring that keeps a large codebase alive.

In recent years I've integrated AI agents into my daily workflow — task breakdown, code generation, testing, regression prevention. Used properly it doesn't replace judgment; it buys back the time to apply it. Faster iteration, same standards.

I studied software engineering at SMK Negeri 4 Bandung, and I still work the way my teachers there drilled into us: understand the requirement before you write the first line. No risk, no story — but no shortcuts either.
